### Option 1 — Full Detail (for Data Analyst / BI Developer roles)

```
• Built a 2-page Power BI dashboard on 91,250 rows of supply chain data, surfacing
  $33.4M in revenue at 33.17% gross margin and identifying 50 SKUs below reorder
  point across 5 warehouses and 10 suppliers.

• Engineered 12 DAX measures using SUMX, CALCULATE, DIVIDE, DISTINCTCOUNT, and
  VAR/RETURN patterns — including Forecast Accuracy % (88.15%), Stockout Rate %,
  Days of Stock Remaining (23.5 days), and Promotion Uplift %.

• Applied 10 Power Query (M) transformations including conditional inventory
  classification (Critical / Low / Healthy) against dynamic reorder point thresholds,
  and date-part columns to fix time-series sort ordering.

• Designed a 2-page interactive dashboard with KPI cards, dual-line demand vs forecast
  charts, supplier lead time comparisons, and inventory status distribution visuals
  with Red / Amber / Green conditional formatting.
```
